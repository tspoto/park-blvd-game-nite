How it works

  1. Vote for games and Saturdays

    Every Saturday of the month is on the ballot, minus holiday weekends. Pick your top three games and every Saturday that might work. Voting isn't a promise to come.

  2. Vote early, update anytime

    Each month's voting opens about five weeks ahead, so you can plan around it. As your schedule firms up, vote again with the same name to update your ballot.

  3. The date gets set

    Voting closes on a Wednesday, 10 days before the month's first Saturday. The Saturday the most people can make wins, and it shows up here along with who's hosting.

  4. Come if you can

    No RSVP needed. If your plans change, that's fine.

  5. Play what fits the table

    We start with the highest-voted game that fits the people who show up. If more people come than a game allows, we partner up. A good problem to have.
